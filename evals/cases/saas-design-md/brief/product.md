# Foldline (fictional product for this evaluation)

Foldline turns meeting notes into a shared list of decisions and follow-up tasks for small teams.

## Capabilities (all shipped)

- Paste or upload meeting notes; Foldline extracts decisions and action items.
- Every action item has an owner and a due date.
- Weekly digest email of open items per team.
- Export to CSV and Markdown.

## Pricing

- Free: 1 team, 20 meetings per month.
- Team: 12 EUR per user per month, billed monthly, unlimited meetings.
- No enterprise plan yet.
