## About

Mehlwerk is a family-run neighbourhood bakery in a fictional district of a mid-sized German
city. We bake with regional flour and a long dough rest (at least 24 hours for all breads).
We opened in 2016 and bake everything on site. Founded and run by Sabine and Jonas Brandt.
